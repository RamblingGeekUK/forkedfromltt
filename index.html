This started as a simple "where do they post now" list. People would notice a host they liked had stopped showing up, go looking, and find nothing — so this was meant to point at the channels and socials those creators were promoting themselves.

In practice it ended up listing far more people than that, including current staff and the company's owners. At that point it stops being a fan directory and starts looking like something else, and I understand why that made people uncomfortable. That was never the intent, but intent isn't really the point.

All listings have been removed.
